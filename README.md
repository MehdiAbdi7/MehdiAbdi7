## Featured projects

### 🍔 [Niwa Food](https://github.com/MehdiAbdi7/niwa-food) · [Live demo](https://niwa-food.vercel.app)

Ordering platform for a fast food with two stores. Customers order from their table with a QR code, for takeaway or for delivery. The kitchen sees each order arrive live, and the staff run everything from a back-office.

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `Redux Toolkit` `Express 5` `MongoDB` `Socket.io` `Zod`

### 💡 [HCA ELEC](https://github.com/MehdiAbdi7/HCA-ELEC) · [Live demo](https://hca-elec.vercel.app)

Online store for electrical and lighting supplies, built for my former company, now closed (the site stays online as a demo). Catalogue filters live in the URL, the cart stores only product IDs, and orders go out as a pre-filled WhatsApp message, with delivery to all 58 wilayas.

`Next.js 16` `TypeScript` `Tailwind CSS v4` `Redux Toolkit` `Zod`

### 🗂️ [Portfolio](https://github.com/MehdiAbdi7/MehdiLabsDZ-portfolio) · [Live site](https://mehdiabdi.netlify.app)

My portfolio, with a separate Express API for the contact form. Scores 98 to 100 on Lighthouse mobile.

`Next.js` `Tailwind CSS` `Motion` `Express`
